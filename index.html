FEATURED PROJECT · EARLY-STAGE PROTOTYPE

🔗 FORMALINK

From Informal Activity to Economic Opportunity

A digital platform prototype exploring how technology and structured data could help informal micro-entrepreneurs better organize their economic activities and prepare progressively for formalization.

Project focus

  • Organizing business activity information.
  • Exploring revenue and expense tracking.
  • Understanding financial management practices.
  • Exploring data visualization and economic analysis.

Research question:

How can technology and data analysis help micro-entrepreneurs better structure their activities and prepare progressively for formalization?

Tools: Python, Streamlit, GitHub and data exploration.
